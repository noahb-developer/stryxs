# Stryxs Strength & Gym Methodology (Part B)

Encode-ready reference for sport-specific, periodized, injury-adaptive strength training for endurance athletes.
Sources: Ronnestad & Mujika 2014 (strength training for endurance, Scand J Med Sci Sports), Lauersen et al. 2014 (BJSM meta-analysis: strength training roughly halves overuse injury risk and cuts acute injuries to about one third), Blagrove et al. 2018 (strength for distance runners, Sports Med: running economy gains 2-8%), Beattie et al. 2014 (strength and endurance performance), Aagaard et al. 2011 (cyclists), Alfredson et al. 1998 (eccentric protocol, Achilles), Beyer et al. 2015 (heavy slow resistance equivalence), McGill 2010 (trunk endurance, the big three), Foster et al. 2001 (session-RPE load), NSCA position guidance on plyometric volume, Boyle (functional progressions).

House rules: every range carries a DEFAULT. Tiers: B (beginner: <1 yr consistent strength work), I (intermediate: 1-3 yr), A (advanced: 3+ yr with barbell competence). All exercises one-line describable. Three equipment variants everywhere: GYM (barbells/machines), MIN (dumbbells + band), BW (bodyweight only).

---

## B1. Why, and what, by sport

What strength training buys an endurance athlete: improved economy (less oxygen per unit of speed/power), injury resistance via tissue capacity, late-race form retention, and for masters athletes, retention of muscle and power that endurance work alone does not protect. Done at low reps and controlled volume alongside endurance work, it does NOT add meaningful mass (Ronnestad & Mujika 2014).

| Sport | Top-3 strength priorities | Top injury sites protected |
|---|---|---|
| Runner (marathon/HM/short) | 1. Calf-soleus capacity (heavy calf raises, bent and straight knee) 2. Hip abductors and glutes 3. Posterior chain + intrinsic foot | Achilles, knee (PFP/ITB), shin, plantar fascia |
| Cyclist | 1. Maximal leg strength (squat/leg press carryover to FTP, Ronnestad) 2. Single-leg strength and stability 3. Trunk endurance for position | Lower back, knee (patellofemoral), neck |
| Swimmer | 1. Shoulder prehab FIRST (rotator cuff + scapular control) 2. Pull strength (lats, serratus) supporting the catch 3. Trunk for streamline | Shoulder, neck, lower back |
| Triathlete | 1. Time-efficient full-body blend 2. Calf capacity + shoulder prehab combined 3. Trunk | Achilles, shoulder, lower back |
| Ultrarunner | 1. Eccentric quad durability (downhill) 2. Calf capacity 3. Trunk and carrying posture (pack/vest) | Quad, knee, ankle, lower back |
| Middle-distance runner | 1. Power and plyometrics (tendon stiffness, Beattie 2014) 2. Hip extension strength 3. Hamstring robustness | Hamstring, Achilles |
| General fitness | Balanced: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry, trunk | Whole-body resilience |

## B2. Session library

All sessions: 30-45 min including the built-in warm-up from Part A section A4.5 (6 min DEFAULT). Load language: RPE 1-10 where RPE 7 means 3 reps left in the tank, RPE 8 means 2 left. %1RM given only as a secondary anchor; the app prescribes RPE. Rest: main lifts 2-3 min DEFAULT 2; accessories 60-90 s DEFAULT 75; circuits 30 s.

### S1. Anatomical Adaptation (intro phase, all sports)
Purpose: tissue prep, movement learning. Duration: weeks 1-3 (B: weeks 1-6). 2 rounds B, 3 rounds I/A, DEFAULT 2. Circuit style, RPE 5-6, 12-15 reps unless noted. ~35 min.
| # | GYM | MIN | BW |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet squat | DB goblet squat | Bodyweight squat, 3 s down |
| 2 | Romanian deadlift (bar) | DB Romanian deadlift | Single-leg hip hinge, hands on hips |
| 3 | Push-up (or machine press) | Push-up | Push-up (incline if needed) |
| 4 | Seated cable row | One-arm DB row | Band row, or towel row on door (OPTIONAL TOOL) |
| 5 | Side plank 30 s/side | same | same |
| 6 | Standing calf raise x15 | same, DB in hand | same, slow |
| 7 | Glute bridge x15 | same | same |

### S2. Max Strength (runners, cyclists, triathletes, ultrarunners)
Purpose: economy and FTP carryover (Ronnestad; Blagrove). 40-45 min. Reps 4-6 at RPE 7-8 (~80-85% 1RM) for mains. B tier substitutes RPE 6-7 and 6-8 reps for the first 4 weeks.
| # | Exercise | Sets x reps | GYM | MIN | BW |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Squat pattern | 4 x 4-6, DEFAULT 4x5 | Back or goblet squat | Heavy DB goblet squat | Rear-foot-elevated split squat, 3 s down |
| 2 | Hinge | 3 x 6 | Romanian deadlift | DB RDL | Single-leg hip hinge, slow |
| 3 | Single-leg | 3 x 6/side | DB split squat | DB split squat | Split squat with pause |
| 4 | Calf, heavy | 3 x 8 (runners/tri: 4 x 8) | Standing calf raise, loaded | DB single-leg calf raise | Single-leg calf raise, 3 s down |
| 5 | Trunk | 3 x 30-45 s | Pallof press (cable) | Band Pallof press | Side plank + bird dog x8/side |
Swimmers do NOT run S2 as written; see S6 ordering rule.

### S3. Power / Plyometric (middle-distance, short-course run, advanced runners and triathletes; NOT for B tier in first 6 weeks)
Purpose: tendon stiffness, rate of force (Beattie 2014). 30-35 min. All jumps: full recovery, quality over quantity, land soft and quiet. Contact caps in B6.
| # | Exercise | Sets x reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Box jump or squat jump (GYM box / MIN-BW: squat jump) | 3 x 5 |
| 2 | Single-leg hops in place | 2 x 8/side |
| 3 | Bounds (exaggerated running strides) | 3 x 10 m |
| 4 | Med-ball chest throw (GYM/MIN) or explosive push-up (BW) | 3 x 5 |
| 5 | Heavy squat pattern from S2 row 1 | 3 x 4 (pairs with jumps for potentiation, A tier only) |
Frequency cap: 1 plyo session/week B-I, 2/week A. DEFAULT 1.

### S4. Maintenance (Build and Peak phases)
Purpose: keep gains at minimal cost. 25-30 min. 1 session/week. 2 sets per exercise of S2 rows 1, 2, 4, 5 at the same load as the last Base-phase session minus one rep per set (RPE 7). Research basis: one weekly session retains strength for 8+ weeks in concurrent training (Ronnestad & Mujika).

### S5. Core / Trunk block (standalone 15-20 min, or appended 8-10 min)
McGill big three plus anti-rotation. All variants identical across equipment tiers.
1. McGill curl-up x8/side (hands under low back, one knee bent)
2. Side plank 3 x 30-45 s/side, DEFAULT 30
3. Bird dog x8/side, 2 s hold
4. Pallof press (band; BW substitute: dead bug x10/side)
5. Dead bug x10/side
Progression: add 5 s to holds or 2 reps per week, cap at 60 s holds.

### S6. Sport prehab blocks (8-12 min, appended to any session or done on easy days)

**Runner calf-shin-foot block** [protects ACH, SHIN, PF]
1. Straight-knee calf raise 3 x 12, slow down phase (gastrocnemius)
2. Bent-knee calf raise 3 x 12 (soleus, the long-distance workhorse)
3. Toe walks 2 x 15 m
4. Foot doming (shorten arch without curling toes) 2 x 10
Load when 3 x 12 is easy: add DB or single-leg.

**Swimmer shoulder block** [protects SHO]. Rule: this block precedes any swimmer upper-body strength work, always, all tiers.
1. Band external rotation, elbow at side, 2 x 15/side (band; BW substitute: side-lying ER with light object)
2. Band pull-aparts 2 x 15
3. Scapular push-ups 2 x 10
4. Wall slides 2 x 8
5. YTW raises on floor or bench 2 x 6 each letter

**Cyclist hip-trunk block** [protects LB, HIP, KNEE]
1. Glute bridge march 2 x 8/side
2. Side plank 2 x 30 s/side
3. Half-kneeling hip flexor isometric: front-foot press-down 2 x 20 s/side
4. Bird dog 2 x 8/side
5. Lateral band walk 2 x 10 steps/side (band; BW: side-lying leg raise x12)

**Runner hip-ITB block** [protects KNEE lateral, HIP]
1. Side-lying hip abduction 2 x 15/side
2. Single-leg glute bridge 2 x 10/side
3. Lateral band walk 2 x 10/side (BW: clamshells x15/side)
4. Single-leg balance, eyes forward, 2 x 30 s/side
5. Slow step-down from stair, 2 x 8/side

**Hamstring robustness block** (middle-distance, sprint-containing personas) [protects HS]
1. Single-leg RDL, slow, 2 x 8/side
2. Hamstring bridge walkout 2 x 6
3. Nordic curl regression (kneeling fall-forward, hands catch) 2 x 4, A tier only; B/I use rows 1-2.

## B3. Periodization into the endurance plan

Strength volume by phase (sessions/week). Endurance-side phase definitions live in each persona doc; this table is the strength side and is persona-agnostic.

| Phase | B | I | A | DEFAULT | Session types |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base early | 2 | 2 | 2-3 | 2 | S1 weeks 1-3 (B: 1-6), then S2 |
| Base late | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | S2 (+S3 where persona allows) |
| Build | 1 | 1-2 | 2 | 1 | S4 maintenance (+1 S2 if 2/week) |
| Peak | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | S4 |
| Taper | 0-1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | S4 at 2 sets, last session per B6 timing |
| Race week | 0 | 0 | 0-1 light | 0 | Movement/prehab only |
| Off-season/transition | 2 | 2-3 | 3 | 2 | S1 then S2, the strength-gaining window |

Prehab blocks (S6) and core (S5) run year-round, 2 x/week, independent of phase. DEFAULT 2.

**Scheduling rules (hard rules):**
1. No strength session within 48 h BEFORE a key endurance session (long run, race-simulation, goal interval session). Strength the day AFTER a key session is fine.
2. Hard days hard: place strength on the same day as a hard endurance day, not on recovery days (preserves easy-day recovery, Seiler polarization logic).
3. Same-day ordering: the priority discipline goes first. For endurance athletes that means endurance first, strength second. Gap: 6+ h ideal, 1 h minimum, DEFAULT same-day with 6 h. Exception: in the off-season strength-gaining window, strength may go first.
4. Runners: never heavy strength the day before the long run, all tiers, no exceptions.
5. Minimum 48 h between heavy lower-body sessions (72 h for masters 50+).

**Counting strength toward weekly load:** use session-RPE (Foster 2001): load AU = RPE x minutes. For TSS-based personas, approximate hTSS at 50-70 per hour, DEFAULT 60/h for S2-S3, 40/h for S1/S4/S5. Strength load counts toward the weekly ramp caps defined in each persona's adaptive rules.

## B4. Injury-adaptive adjustment (codeable mapping)

Trigger: athlete reports a niggle in chat or workout feedback. Pipeline: (1) red-flag screen (B5), (2) if clear, apply the row below: REMOVE/reduce, INSERT block, set check-in, (3) run the decision tree.

Global pain rule, app-wide, every row below inherits it: training may continue only if pain is 3/10 or less, does NOT worsen during activity, and settles within 24 h. Pain above 3/10, or rising during the session, means stop the session. (Standard tendinopathy pain-monitoring model, Silbernagel.)

| Site x sport | Likely pattern | REMOVE / reduce this week | INSERT block (exact content from B2/B6) | Duration | Check-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Front of knee, running | Patellofemoral pain | Downhill running, speed work, stairs under load. Keep easy volume if pain rule passes. | Runner hip-ITB block 3 x/week + wall-sit isometric 3 x 30-45 s + slow step-downs 2 x 8/side | 4-6 wks, DEFAULT 4 | 1 wk |
| Outside of knee, running | ITB syndrome | Downhill and cambered roads; cut long run 30% | Runner hip-ITB block 3 x/week | 4-6 wks, DEFAULT 6 | 1 wk |
| Knee, cycling | Patellofemoral / fit | Big-gear low-cadence work; flag saddle-height/fit check (often too low or too far forward) | Cyclist hip-trunk block 3 x/week; cadence floor 85 rpm this week | 3-4 wks, DEFAULT 3 | 1 wk |
| Achilles / lower calf, running | Achilles tendinopathy | Hill sprints, plyos, speed work; flat easy running only if pain rule passes; no zero-drop shoe changes now | Calf loading: bent + straight knee calf raises 3 x 15 slow, daily morning + heavy slow 3 x/week (Alfredson 1998 / Beyer 2015 heavy-slow equivalence) | 6-12 wks, DEFAULT 8 | 1 wk |
| Calf belly, running | Strain vs overload | If sudden sharp onset: treat as strain, stop running 5-7 days, walk-test. If gradual: reduce volume 30% | Runner calf-shin block 3 x/week, isometric calf holds first 2 wks | 3-6 wks, DEFAULT 4 | 1 wk |
| Shin, inner edge, running | Medial tibial stress syndrome | Run volume minus 30-50% DEFAULT 40%, no speed, soft surfaces. If pain is FOCAL (one fingertip spot on bone): suspect stress fracture, STOP and refer | Runner calf-shin block 3 x/week + foot doming daily | 4-6 wks, DEFAULT 6 | 1 wk |
| Heel / arch, morning-worst, running | Plantar fasciopathy | Reduce volume 20-30%, no barefoot strides | Foot doming daily + calf raise with toes elevated on rolled towel 3 x 12, 3 x/week | 6-12 wks, DEFAULT 8 | 1 wk |
| Hip flexor, cycling | Hip flexor overload / fit | Big-gear work, aggressive aero time; flag fit check (saddle too high or cockpit too long) | Cyclist hip-trunk block 3 x/week with the hip-flexor isometric doubled (4 x 20 s) | 3 wks | 1 wk |
| Outside of hip, running | Gluteal tendinopathy | Crossing midline (narrow-line running), cambered roads, sitting cross-legged, stretching the site (do NOT stretch it) | Side-lying abduction isometrics 3 x 30 s progressing to lateral band walks + single-leg bridge, 3 x/week | 6-9 wks, DEFAULT 6 | 1 wk |
| Lower back, cycling or running | Extension/flexion intolerance, trunk endurance deficit | Loaded gym flexion (deadlifts from floor), long aero blocks; flag bike fit if cycling | S5 core block daily 10 min (McGill big three emphasis) | 3-4 wks, DEFAULT 3 | 1 wk |
| Shoulder, swimming | Subacromial / rotator cuff overload | Paddles, pull buoy + paddle sets, butterfly; cut swim volume 20-30% DEFAULT 25%; flag technique check: hand crossing midline at entry, low elbow | Swimmer shoulder block 3 x/week (not daily; tissue needs recovery between) | 4-6 wks, DEFAULT 4 | 1 wk |
| Neck, cycling | Aero-position extension overload | Continuous aero time over 20 min blocks; raise stack temporarily if possible | Chin tucks 3 x 10 daily + scapular push-ups 2 x 10 + band pull-aparts 2 x 15, 3 x/week | 3 wks | 1 wk |
| Hamstring, running | Strain (sudden) vs tendinopathy (gradual, sit-bone) | Sudden sharp during stride/sprint: stop running 7 days minimum; if walking hurts, refer out. Gradual high/sit-bone: remove speed and hills, keep easy if pain rule passes; do not stretch it | Hamstring robustness block 3 x/week starting with isometric bridge holds 3 x 30 s in week 1 | Strain 3-6 wks DEFAULT 4; tendinopathy 8-12 wks DEFAULT 8 | 1 wk |

**Check-in cadence and decision tree (uniform, all rows):**
- Coach asks at day 7: "Better, same, or worse?"
- BETTER: continue the insert block 2 more weeks. Reintroduce removed elements one at a time, lowest-load first, one per week. Return to normal plan when pain-free 7 consecutive days through a full normal week.
- SAME: keep the block, reduce the aggravating discipline a further 20%, recheck in 1 week. If still SAME at week 2 check-in: recommend a professional assessment while continuing the block.
- WORSE at any check-in, or pain rule violated twice in one week: stop the aggravating activity entirely and refer out (B5 language). Cross-train pain-free disciplines only.

## B5. Red flags: STOP and refer (the app never coaches through these)

If ANY of the following are reported, Coach must stop prescribing loading for that issue, say plainly that this needs a medical professional (physio, sports doctor), and offer pain-free cross-training only:
1. Pain above 3/10 during activity, or pain that worsens as the session goes on
2. Night pain or pain at rest
3. Focal bone tenderness (one fingertip spot), especially shin, foot, hip: stress-fracture pattern
4. Visible swelling, joint locking, or giving way
5. Numbness, tingling, or pain radiating down a limb
6. A pop or snap at onset, or inability to walk without limping
7. Any chest pain, unusual breathlessness, or fainting: stop everything, seek medical care now
8. Symptoms not improving after 2 weeks of modified load (the SAME-SAME branch above)
Tone rule: direct and calm, no diagnosis language. Coach describes patterns ("this pattern is one a physio should look at"), never names a diagnosis as fact.

## B6. Safety and progression guardrails

| Rule | B | I | A | DEFAULT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Load progression per week (same sets x reps) | +2.5% or smallest increment | +2.5-5% | +2.5-5% | +2.5% |
| On-ramp: first 3 weeks of any new phase | RPE cap 6, no failure | RPE cap 7 | RPE cap 7 | RPE 6 |
| Plyometric ground contacts per session | 40-60 | 60-100 | 100-140 | 60 (NSCA guidance) |
| Plyo sessions/week | 1 | 1 | 1-2 | 1 |
| Time between heavy lower-body sessions | 48 h | 48 h | 48 h (72 h masters 50+) | 48 h |
| Reps in reserve floor on main lifts | 3 | 2 | 1-2 | 2 |

Masters modifications (50+): warm-up Raise +50% (Part A), 72 h between heavy sessions, keep 2-3 reps in reserve always, prioritize power work (fast concentric, light-moderate load) once per week year-round since power declines fastest with age.

DO-NOT list (hard rules, all tiers):
1. No max-effort lifts (RPE 9+) within 7 days of any race; within 14 days of marathon-distance or longer.
2. No NEW exercises introduced in taper or race week.
3. No heavy eccentric work or plyometrics within 14 days of a goal running race (DOMS from unaccustomed eccentrics impairs economy for up to 7-10 days).
4. No strength session within 48 h before a key endurance session.
5. No training through any B5 red flag.
6. No strength PR attempts during Build or Peak; PRs belong to the off-season window.
7. Beginners: no barbell back squat or conventional deadlift from floor until 6 weeks of goblet/RDL competence.
