# Coach Reference: short_run (5K/10K) + general (fitness/health)

> [5K10K] = `short_run` (Daniels VDOT + Seiler + Canova; race a target time at mile/5K/10K).
> [GENERAL] = `general` (Attia longevity: Zone 2 + VO2max + strength + stability; usually NO race).
> Gathered 2026-05-23 (round 1). [5K10K vs GENERAL] tagged. Compact codeable summary.

## 0. Shared
Riegel exp 1.06 (1.07 trained); predict only within 0.5-3x of known distance. Daniels-Gilbert VDOT formulas (see marathon ref §3). Seiler: >=80% easy / <=20% hard / <=10% grey zone. LT1=2mmol (top of true easy / Attia Zone 2 ceiling), LT2=4mmol (~LTHR).
**Core rule (both):** most volume genuinely easy, a minority genuinely hard, almost nothing in the grey middle.

## 1. STRUCTURE
[5K10K] base->build->peak->1wk taper->race. Shorter race = more I (VO2max) + R (speed), less T/M. Quality mix by event: 10k T0.35/I0.45/R0.20; 5k T0.25/I0.50/R0.25; mile T0.15/I0.45/R0.40 (marathon was T0.55/I0.30/R0.15). Phase by weeks: 8→1/3/3/1, 12→3/4/4/1, 16+→extend base/5/5/1. Taper ALWAYS 1 wk.
[GENERAL] NO phases-to-date. Rolling pillars (stability daily, Zone 2 continuous, strength 2-4x, VO2max ~1x) + 4-wk rotation to avoid staleness: A=Zone2 vol up, B=strength intensity up, C=VO2max block (2/wk, ≤6-8wk), D=deload+retest. Set 8-12 week "health goals" (measurable on one pillar), not race dates. NEVER zero any pillar.

## 2. WEEKLY PROGRESSION
[5K10K] mileage-based: ≤10%/wk volume, deload every 3-4 wks (cut 25-35%), long run ≤30% weekly (≤90-100min), ≥1 easy/rest between hard, 2 hard/wk typical (3 only advanced build/peak). By level: beginner 3-4 sess/20-35km/1-2 hard; intermediate 4-5/40-60/2; advanced 6-7/65-110/2-3.
[GENERAL] Attia template (hours+sessions, not km): Zone 2 ≥3h/wk floor (ideal 4), 1 VO2max/wk (2 only in Block C ≤6-8wk), strength 2-4x (never <2), stability 10-15min most days. Deload every 4-6wk (cut 30-40%) then retest. Targets by time: crunched 3-4h (2-2.5 Z2, 1 VO2, 2 strength); standard 5-6h (3-3.5 Z2, 1 VO2, 3 strength); high 7-9h.

## 3. ZONES
[5K10K] Daniels E/M/T/I/R (emphasize I+R). HR from LTHR (Friel run zones). I = 3-5min reps @95-100% VO2max; R = 30-120s speed.
[GENERAL] **Zone-2 naming trap: Attia 5-zone "Zone 2" (low aerobic, AT LT1) == Seiler 3-zone "Zone 1" (low). NOT Seiler's Zone 2 (threshold grey). Always disambiguate.** Attia Zone 2: %HRmax 70-80, %LTHR 80-88, lactate 1.7-2.0, "full sentences slightly labored / nasal-breathing sustainable." VO2max zone: %HRmax 90-95, %LTHR 98-110, lactate >4, "hard, 3-5min repeatable." Find Zone 2 w/o lab = talk test (fastest pace you can speak a full sentence), cap by EARLY-session HR (drift).

## 4. SESSIONS
[5K10K] Easy (30-90min E). Long (60-100min E, ≤30% wk). Tempo (20-30min T or 4-6x5min cruise). **VO2max (key): 5-6x3min @I / 5-6x1000m / 6-8x800m / 8-12x400m, equal jog; total I ≤8% weekly.** Reps (8-12x200-400m @R, full recovery 2.5x). Strides 6-8x20s. Hills (early base).
[GENERAL] Zone 2 steady (any modality, 30-60+min, aim 45-60). **VO2max: Norwegian 4x4 — 4x4min @90-95% HRmax, 3min active recovery @65-72%** (any modality; alts 5x3, 6x2). Strength compound: strength 80-90%1RM 3-5x3-6 (Attia default), hypertrophy 67-80% 3-4x8-12, endurance <67% 12-20. Movements: squat/hinge/push/pull/carry/single-leg. Stability daily 10-15min (single-leg balance, Pallof, hinge, ankle/T-spine). Rucking/incline-walk = great low-impact Zone 2.

## 5. ADAPTATION
Keystone (never drop): [5K10K] the I session; [GENERAL] strength (≥2x) + Zone 2 (≥3h). By days: 2d→1 quality+1 long [5K10K] / 1 Z2+1 strength [GEN]; 3d→I+T+easy / 2 Z2+1 strength; 4d→2 quality+2 easy / 2 Z2+1 strength+1 VO2; 5d+→ scale.
[GENERAL] AGE (Attia, masters 50+): stability non-negotiable (fall prevention top-3 lever), keep heavy loading (bone), keep VO2max 1x (strongest mortality predictor) scaled, recovery 2 days between hard. Modality substitution: VO2max is intensity-defined (90-95% HRmax 3-5min, ANY modality), Zone 2 is HR/talk-defined (ANY sustainable modality); only run economy needs running ([5K10K] only).

## 6. TAPER/TEST
[5K10K] 1-wk taper, cut volume 40-60% keep intensity; last hard touch 3-4 days out (small); over-tapering flattens short-distance.
[GENERAL] no taper; test on deload every 8-12 wks: VO2max test (Cooper 12min / 1.5mi / ramp), Zone 2 benchmark (hold fixed HR 20-30min, record pace/power), strength (Epley 1RM = w*(1+reps/30)). Fresh, one test/day.

## 7. RACE/TEST DAY
[5K10K] run near threshold/VO2 → WARMUP matters (15-20min E + drills + strides + pickups, finish near gun), fueling minimal (none in-race). 5K: controlled start, settle by 1km, lift last km. 10K: conservative first 2km, negative/even split.
[GENERAL] periodic test: full WU, ONE test, measure distance/time + peak HR + 1-min recovery HR; older/new → submaximal (Rockport walk/ramp), never max if new/ill/unscreened.

## 8. PROGRESS / PROJECTION
[5K10K] predict via VDOT + Riegel (avg), within 0.5-3x; range ±2-3% same ballpark, ±4-5% near 3x. Predicting UP in distance assumes endurance volume (flag).
[GENERAL] NO finish time → fitness progress panel:
- **Est VO2max:** Cooper `(m-504.9)/44.73`; 1.5mi `88.02-2.76*t_min`; Rockport `132.853-0.0769w-0.3877age+6.315sex-3.2649t-0.1565hr`; HR-ratio (Uth) `15.3*(HRmax/HRrest)`.
- VO2max norms (ml/kg/min) by age/sex (e.g. M30-39 poor<34/good40-47/excellent>47; F30-39 poor<28/good33-38). 
- Zone 2 pace/power at fixed HR (rises over 6-8wk = primary aerobic signal), resting HR 7-day trend (falling=fitter), est 1RM trend, "fitness age" (map VO2max to the age where it's average).

## 9. ANTI-PATTERNS
[5K10K] DON'T: race the workouts (I=95-100% not all-out), neglect easy (≥80% easy), spike speedwork (I ≤8% wk, add 1 quality before a 2nd), two hard back-to-back, skip warmup, >10%/wk volume.
[GENERAL] DON'T: live in the GREY ZONE (medium-hard everything — keep <=10% time LT1-LT2; easy truly easy, hard truly hard 90-95% HRmax) [the #1 failure], neglect strength (≥2x always), only-HIIT no Zone2 (≥3h Z2 floor), ego-lift, ignore recovery (separate VO2max+heavy strength ≥1 day, ≥2 masters; deload 4-6wk), drop stability (daily, esp masters).

## 10. FEASIBILITY
[5K10K] VDOT gain/8-12wk block: beginner 3-5, intermediate 2-3, trained 1-2. Volume floor by goal: mile rec 25-40/comp 50-80; 5K rec 30-50/comp 60-90; 10K rec 40-60/comp 70-110 km/wk. If gap>max gain for weeks available → tell truth, propose realistic time/timeline. Below volume floor → pace may exist but endurance to hold it doesn't.
[GENERAL] gain rates/8-12wk: VO2max beginner +15-20% / trained +1-5%; strength beginner +20-40% / trained +2-8%; Z2 efficiency noticeable 6-8wk beginner; RHR drops several bpm 4-8wk. Set ONE primary measurable goal (Z2 pace ↑, VO2max +2-4, 1RM +10-20% beginner, fitness age −N), maintain all pillars; scale down if requested gain > max rate.

## Cheat sheet
[5K10K]: VDOT brain, race a time, base→build→peak→1wk taper, weekly km, emphasize I+R, key session 5-6x3min@I, predict via VDOT+Riegel, top risk = over-racing workouts, running-specific.
[GENERAL]: Attia brain, no race, rolling pillars + 4wk cycle + 8-12wk health goals, weekly hours+sessions, Zone2 floor + 1 VO2max + strength + stability, key session Norwegian 4x4, progress via est VO2max / Z2-pace-at-HR / RHR / 1RM / fitness age, top risk = grey zone + dropping strength/stability, any modality.
