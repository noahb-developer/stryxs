## F. IN-SESSION STRUCTURE AND PHASE SCALING

Universal template: WU (purpose: raise temperature, groove stroke) -> PS pre-set (activate the day's pace or skill) -> MS main set -> CD (flush). WU and CD scale with tier: beginner WU 200 to 300, intermediate 300 to 400, advanced 400 to 600; CD 100 to 300 all tiers. Masters 50 plus: WU x1.5 (Part 3, B7).

Send-off rule (restated with the Part 3 A1 fix): sendoff = ceil((rep_distance / 100 x target_pace + rest) / 5) x 5; if sendoff − swim_time < rest − 4, add 5 s; rest floor 5 s. Standard rest by zone: Z2 = 15 s per 100 of rep distance, Z3 = 12 s, Z4 = 10 to 15 s flat per rep (tight on purpose), Z5 = roughly equal to swim time (1:1), Z6 = 2 to 4 x swim time (full recovery) except USRPT at 15 to 20 s.

### F1. CSS threshold session growth, Base -> Build -> Peak (intermediate, CSS 1:40, 25 m pool, [DISTANCE])

| Phase | WU | PS | MS | CD | Total | Send-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 300 mixed | 4 x 50 descend to CSS, 15 s | 8 x 100 at CSS, 15 s rest | 200 | 2,200 m | 100s on 1:55 |
| Build early | 400 | 4 x 50 descend | 10 x 100 at CSS, 12 s rest | 200 | 2,600 m | 100s on 1:55 (rounding holds) |
| Build late | 400 | 4 x 50 | 5 x 200 at CSS, 20 s rest | 200 | 2,800 m | 200s on 3:40 |
| Peak | 400 | 4 x 50 | 3 x 400 at CSS + 1, 30 s rest, then 4 x 50 at CSS − 4 | 200 | 3,200 m | 400s on 7:15 |

Scaling rules: grow MS volume max 15 percent per step; progress rest DOWN (15 -> 12 -> 10 s) OR rep length UP (100 -> 200 -> 400), never both in the same week. [SPEED] variant: cap CSS reps at 200 m and add the Z6 pre-set earlier in Build.

### F2. VO2 session growth (intermediate, CSS 1:40)

| Phase | MS | Send-off | Total session |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base (touch only) | 6 x 50 at CSS − 4, 1:1 rest | 50s on 1:35 | 1,700 m |
| Build | 8 x 50 at CSS − 5 plus 4 x 100 at CSS − 3 | 50s on 1:35, 100s on 3:15 | 2,100 m |
| Peak | 6 x 100 at CSS − 4, 1:1 rest | 100s on 3:15 | 2,200 m |

### F3. Remaining session types: scale by the same two levers (MS volume +15 percent max per step, rest tightens one notch per phase). Long swim growth is governed by Part 1 (max +500 m per week, 25 to 35 percent of weekly meters). USRPT sets scale by adding 2 to 4 reps per week at FIXED pace and rest, never by pace creep (Rushall: pace is the constant, rep survival is the progression).

## G. WEEKLY TEMPLATES (exact 7-day layouts)

Spacing rules baked in: key session freshest day; 48 h between Z4 plus; long swim never the day after the hardest session; rest or recovery after the two hardest days; no consecutive paddle days. Swap days symmetrically if the athlete's availability differs; preserve gaps, not weekdays.

### [DISTANCE] block

| Sessions/wk | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | rest | CSS session | rest | rest | rest | Long swim with drill WU | rest |
| 3 | rest | CSS | rest | Technique/recovery | rest | Long | rest |
| 4 | rest | CSS | Technique/recovery | rest | Aerobic (pull emphasis) | Long | rest |
| 5 | rest | CSS | Recovery/technique | VO2 (Build/Peak) or 2nd aerobic (Base) | Aerobic pull | Long | rest |
| 6 | Recovery swim | CSS | Technique | VO2 or 2nd CSS (Peak) | Aerobic pull | Long | rest |

Phase notes: Base replaces VO2 slot with aerobic; Peak may swap Friday aerobic for an OW-skills session when the event is open water (2 x per week OW skills in Peak per Part 1, 4.11: the second one folds into the long swim as sighting plus pace-change inserts).

### [SPEED] block

| Sessions/wk | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | rest | Race-pace (USRPT) | rest | rest | rest | CSS plus speed PS | rest |
| 3 | rest | Race-pace | rest | Technique/recovery | rest | CSS | rest |
| 4 | rest | Race-pace | Technique/recovery | rest | VO2 | Aerobic with starts/turns block | rest |
| 5 | rest | Race-pace | Recovery | VO2 | Technique | Aerobic plus race-pace touches | rest |
| 6 | Recovery | Race-pace | Technique | VO2 | Race-pace 2 (Peak only, else aerobic) | Aerobic, starts and turns | rest |

Phase notes: Base converts one race-pace slot to CSS; Peak holds two race-pace sessions (never consecutive days, AS rules apply); starts and turns work lives inside Saturday all phases.

DEFAULT template if goal unknown: [DISTANCE] table, [FITNESS] athletes use it with the long swim capped at 25 percent of weekly meters and the VO2 slot always aerobic.

## H. PROJECTION DEPTH (worked examples, all computed, unit-test anchors)

Efficiency modifier rule (new, codeable): if the athlete fails the stroke-count gate for their fitness tier (Part 2, 5.2), use ONE TIER LOWER for the fade-offset column (8.2) and the OW conversion column (8.3). Fitness sets the CSS; efficiency sets how well it holds over distance and in open water.

### H-A. Efficient vs inefficient at the same fitness
Both intermediate, CSS 105.0 s (1:45). Swimmer E: count 16 per 25 (passes gate). Swimmer I: count 23 (fails gate, beginner columns apply).
- 1500 m pool: E = (105 + 2) x 15 = 1605.0 s = 26:45.0. I = (105 + 4) x 15 = 1635.0 s = 27:15.0. Gap 30.0 s.
- 3.8 km OW, calm, no wetsuit: E = (105 + 4 + 4) x 38 = 4294.0 s = 1:11:34.0. I = (105 + 7 + 5) x 38 = 4446.0 s = 1:14:06.0. Gap 152.0 s, about 2.5 minutes from efficiency alone.
Coach takeaway encoded: for swimmer I, technique work IS race preparation, worth more than any fitness block.

### H-B. No CSS test, pace plus RPE only
Six logged steady swims (800 m plus each, RPE 4 to 5), average 125.0 s per 100 (2:05). Provisional CSS = 125 − 9 = 116.0 s (1:56). Tier by data: intermediate volume, gate unknown -> intermediate fade, LOW confidence forced (Part 2, 8.5).
- 1500 m pool = (116 + 2) x 15 = 1770.0 s = 29:30.0, range plus or minus 8 percent = 1628.4 to 1911.6 s = 27:08.4 to 31:51.6.
App behavior: deliver the projection with the LOW label and schedule a 400/200 test within 10 days (Part 2, 5.2).

### H-C. Mid-block CSS update
Part 2 athlete (CSS 100.0) retests in week 6: 400 m in 6:17 (377 s), 200 m in 3:01 (181 s).
- New CS = 200 / (377 − 181) = 1.0204 m/s. New CSS = (377 − 181) / 2 = 98.0 s. New D' = 400 − 1.0204 x 377 = 15.31 m (passes the A3 clamp, 5 to 40).
- New 1500 m = (98 + 2) x 15 = 1500.0 s = 25:00.0 (was 25:30.0, gain 30.0 s). New 100 m via CS/D' = (100 − 15.31) / 1.0204 = 83.0 s.
Update rules: projections switch to the new CSS immediately, no blending; all zone paces and send-offs re-derive (AS16); improvement of 2.0 s per 100 in 6 weeks sits at the top of the intermediate rate band (Part 2, section 10), so the feasibility engine also re-runs.

### H-D. 25 m vs 50 m pool
CSS tested in 25 m pool = 100.0 s. 1500 m in the 25 m pool = (100 + 2) x 15 = 1530.0 s = 25:30.0. Same swimmer, 50 m pool: CSS_50eq = 100 + 1.5 = 101.5, so (101.5 + 2) x 15 = 1552.5 s = 25:52.5. Difference 22.5 s. The app must therefore always know the EVENT pool length, not just the training pool length.

### H-E. Pool 1500 vs open-water 1500 (same intermediate, CSS 100.0)
- Pool (25 m): 1530.0 s = 25:30.0.
- OW calm, no wetsuit: (100 + 2 + 4) x 15 = 1590.0 s = 26:30.0.
- OW calm, wetsuit: (100 + 2 + 4 − 4) x 15 = 1530.0 s = 25:30.0. For an intermediate, the wetsuit benefit exactly cancels the calm-OW penalty (by construction of the 8.3 defaults); beginners come out ahead in a wetsuit (−6 vs +5), advanced slightly behind (−2.5 vs +3).

Unit-test anchors: 1605.0, 1635.0, 4294.0, 4446.0, 1770.0, 1628.4, 1911.6, 98.0, 1.0204, 15.31, 1500.0, 83.0, 1552.5, 22.5, 1590.0, 1530.0.

## I. ENVIRONMENT AND LOGISTICS

### I1. Water temperature (open water)

| Temp C | Rule |
|---|---|
| under 12 | DO NOT race or train solo; events at this temp are specialist territory, app declines to plan them below advanced tier |
| 12 to 16 | wetsuit MANDATORY in plans, swims capped 45 min beginner / 75 min others, cold-shock protocol below |
| 16 to 18 | wetsuit strongly defaulted, pace +2 s per 100 expectation (Part 2, 8.3) |
| 18 to 24 | athlete choice where legal; wetsuit usually faster (8.3 benefits) |
| over 24.5 | wetsuit typically illegal (standard event rule) and overheating risk; hydrate, expect +1 to 2 s per 100 above 28 C |

Cold-shock protocol (every cold OW swim): enter slowly, face in, exhale-controlled for 60 to 90 s before swimming off; never dive into cold water unwarmed; hypothermia abort signs the app must state: claw hand, slurred thinking, sudden warmth feeling. Why: cold-shock gasp reflex, not fitness, is the dominant OW fatality mechanism.

### I2. Chop, current, sighting lines: pace by RPE not clock in rough water (Part 2 section 7); shorten stroke and raise SR +3 to 5 spm into chop; breathe away from waves; sight on a high fixed landmark behind the buoy, not the buoy. Current: swim the perpendicular-plus-offset line, aim upstream of the target by the drift angle; if the event has a known current leg, add the 8.3 factor to that leg only.

### I3. Pack and draft positioning: feet-draft saves 5 to 10 percent energy, hip-draft 6 to 10 percent with cleaner water (preferred when available). Position rule by seed: only start front-center if seeded in the top 20 percent of predicted pace, else start wide and 2 to 3 rows back, swim 10 to 20 m extra for clean water (cheaper than the washing machine). Practice drafting 1 x per week in Peak when OW access exists.

### I4. Pool logistics: lane sharing splits or circles change rest reality, allow send-off +5 s in crowded sessions; pool water over 29 C degrades hard-session quality, swap VO2 for technique; 50 m pool sessions use min 50 m reps (Part 1).

### I5. Altitude: events above 1,500 m, add +1.5 s per 100 to all race paces per 1,000 m above that (DEFAULT, sparse swimming-specific data, conservative); arrival either under 48 h or over 10 days before racing.

### I6. Event week and morning checklist (the app renders this as a card): wetsuit worn 3 plus times before race day, anti-chafe at neck and underarms, two pairs of tested goggles (tinted plus clear), feeds rehearsed for 75 min plus events (30 to 60 g carbs per hour, Part 2), course map sighting landmarks chosen the day before, activation swim per Part 2 section 6, race-morning warmup 600 to 1,200 m where water access allows, else 10 min land warmup with bands and arm swings ending 20 min before the start, pacing plan stated as a per-100 target plus a first-400 cap.

## J. DRYLAND STRENGTH (shoulder-health first)

2 x per week, 20 to 35 min, year-round; mandatory for masters 50 plus and post-AS2 athletes. Exercise menu: prehab = band external rotation, band W and Y, scapular wall slides, side-lying ER; catch-support = pull-ups or lat pulldown, straight-arm pulldown, serratus punches, plank rows; core and streamline = dead bug, hollow hold, pallof press, overhead squat reach; mobility = thoracic extensions over roller, pec doorway stretch, ankle plantar-flexion stretch (kick flexibility).

| Phase | Prehab | Catch-support strength | Core/streamline | Loading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 2 x 15 each, every session | 3 x 10 at RPE 6 | 3 x 30 to 45 s | learn movements, light |
| Build | 2 x 15 maintained | 4 x 6 to 8 at RPE 8 | 3 x 45 s | progressive overload, +2.5 to 5 percent per week |
| Peak | 2 x 15 maintained | 2 x 5 fast-intent at RPE 7 | 2 x 30 s | maintain, cut volume 40 percent |
| Taper | 1 x 15, final session 5 plus days out | none in final 5 days | light | shed |

Rules: dryland never the morning before a key swim; pull-up and pulldown volume counts 0.5 x into shoulder_load_units per set of 10 (add to the Part 1 formula); thoracic and ankle mobility daily 5 min is the cheapest speed in the program for desk workers (streamline and kick angle). Impingement red flag: painful arc 60 to 120 degrees on raises = AS2 path.

## K. PROACTIVE COACHING TRIGGERS (SP1 to SP15: condition -> message intent)

- SP1 CSS-set fade > 3 s per 100 in EVERY CSS session for 2 weeks: explain fade, announce the AS7/AS8 adjustment, reassure that pace is being protected over volume.
- SP2 Stroke-count creep flag (AS6) fired: explain technique-breakdown-under-fatigue in one line, tell them which drill block is being inserted and the expected gain (B1 table numbers).
- SP3 Long swim missed 2 consecutive weeks (AS10 second clause): ask the one scheduling question, offer the split-long-swim fallback (two 60 percent swims) for one cycle.
- SP4 CSS stalled: no retest improvement across 2 retests at intermediate plus: point to the efficiency metrics trend (C1), propose shifting 10 points of volume to technique or race-pace depending on goal, set the expectation from the Part 2 improvement-rate table.
- SP5 ACWR spike clamped (AS3): tell them the plan was clamped and why, in plain words, BEFORE they notice the week shrank.
- SP6 Shoulder soreness recurring (AS2 second flag): firm message: paddles gone, volume cut, prehab block added, see a professional if pain passes 3 of 10 or wakes them.
- SP7 New CSS or race-distance PB (AS15/AS16 path): congratulate with the specific number and the new projections, name which lever produced it (fitness vs efficiency per C1).
- SP8 Taper started: explain that feeling flat or twitchy mid-taper is normal and expected, volume is down X percent by design, trust the freshening curve (TSB rising).
- SP9 Event in 7 days: deliver the I6 checklist card and the pacing plan with the first-400 cap.
- SP10 OW event within 6 weeks and zero OW swims logged: warn about the confidence-level penalty (Part 2, 8.5) and prescribe the graduated OW exposure protocol (B10), or the pool OW-skills fallback (5.4).
- SP11 Wetsuit-legal event, wetsuit owned, zero wetsuit swims by 3 weeks out: schedule the 3 mandatory wetsuit swims (5.4) now.
- SP12 Sick tag (AS1): empathy plus the concrete return ramp, no heroics framing.
- SP13 Athlete exceeding plan 2 weeks (AS14): acknowledge, advance the ramp one step, restate the caps that still bind and why.
- SP14 Demonstrated availability below plan (AS13): non-judgmental regeneration offer at the real availability; a 3-day plan completed beats a 5-day plan abandoned.
- SP15 First swim ever logged in the app, or return after 14 plus days: welcome or welcome-back, restart rule from anti-pattern 2 (60 percent restart) stated as protection, not punishment.

Message style per house rules: direct, kind, no exclamation overload, no em-dashes, truth over flattery.
